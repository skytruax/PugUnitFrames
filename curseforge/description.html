PitBull-style unit frames for Forever. Lighter, and ready the moment you log in.

If you miss PitBull on Forever, start here. Free Unit Frames gives you the clean, class-coloured frames PitBull players build for themselves (player, target, focus, pet, party and raid) without an evening in the options, and it is written from scratch for the way Forever fights.

Everything a frame should tell you is there, and nothing shouts. Bars are coloured by class or by friend and foe, numbers are written the way you like them, and the things that matter in a fight (who is being hit, who is out of reach, whom you can help) show up on the frame itself. It is one small folder with no libraries in it.

Why you will like it

Seven frames, yours to arrange

Player, target, target of target, focus, pet, party and raid. Switch each one off, size it, scale it, and put it where you want it. The player, target, focus and pet have their own cast bars; the target shows its buffs and debuffs, and your combo points if you are a rogue or a druid. Shamans get their totems under the player frame.

Your group, at a glance

Party and raid frames tell you what you need without a second look: an outline on whoever is being attacked, healing on its way drawn past the end of the health bar, a frame lit in the colour of a debuff you can remove, and members out of range faded. Raid frames come for 10, 20 or 40 players and are drawn larger in a small raid. Ready checks, raid icons, the leader's crown and a resurrection on its way all show on the frame.

Built for how Forever fights

In a fight, Forever seals much of what a unit frame shows: an addon is handed health, names, casts and auras that it can pass on to be drawn and may not read. Free Unit Frames is written around that from the first line, so the frames keep drawing in the middle of a fight, and someone who joins your group mid-pull gets a working frame at once. It includes no library and no other addon's code.

Buffs, debuffs and cast bars

Icons show their time left and their stacks. Debuffs are rimmed in the colour of their kind (magic, curse, disease, poison), and on your target you can show only your own. Put the icons above, below or beside a frame, or on the frame itself. A cast bar fills for a cast and drains for a channel with a bright edge on the end of its fill, shows a shield when the cast cannot be interrupted, and flashes red when it is.

Three looks, three textures

Slate, Night and Hearth, each with smooth, flat or glass bars, all drawn for this addon. The frames are lettered in Inter, a clean typeface the addon brings with it (the game's own font is one setting away), and every plate casts a soft shadow, so the frames stand off whatever is behind them. The empty part of a bar keeps the bar's colour, darkened, so you still read a class at a glance when someone is nearly dead.

Colour health by class, by friend or foe, by how much is left, or keep it plain. Write health and power as exact numbers, short numbers, a percentage, or as what is missing.

Unlock, drag, done

Type /freeuf unlock and a green handle appears on every frame: drag one to move its frame, then /freeuf lock. Everything else is in the options, frame by frame. Profiles can be copied, switched per character, and shared as text.

Getting started

Type /freeuf for the options.

  • /freeuf unlock shows the handles; /freeuf lock puts them away.
  • /freeuf reset player (or target, targettarget, focus, pet, party, raid, all) puts a frame back where and how it started.
  • /freeuf profile gives you your settings as text, and takes someone else's.
  • /freeuf status tells you the version and lists any error the addon caught. That is what to send when something looks wrong.

Click a frame to target, right-click for the menu. Click-casting addons find these frames where they look for them.

Good to know

  • The game's own unit frames and cast bar step aside for these. Each can be kept: Options > AddOns > Free Unit Frames.
  • The game protects unit frames while you fight. They can be moved and sized out of combat; a change you make in a fight happens when it ends.
  • It leaves the game's menus alone and hooks none of the game's code, with controller play in mind.
  • English, German, Spanish and French. Where the game runs in Korean or Chinese, the frames are lettered in the game's own font.
  • It is not a copy of PitBull and shares no code with it: there are no text tags to script and no modules to switch on. It has the settings most players reach for, frame by frame, and more are on the way.
  • This is the first version. Reports are welcome, with the text of /freeuf status.
